========================================
GOAL
====

Make Muqarrar AI:

* more natural (less robotic)
* more user-friendly

========================================

1. RESPONSE STYLE IMPROVEMENT
   ========================================

Currently, answers tend to start with phrases like:
"Berdasarkan kitab..." or "Dalam muqarrar ini..."

We want to change this.

NEW RULE:

* Do NOT mention the kitab at the beginning of the answer
* Start directly with the explanation (natural tone)
* Mention the source ONLY in the "Rujukan" section at the end

Example change:

OLD:
"Berdasarkan Fathul Qarib..."

NEW:
"Najis mutawassithah adalah..."

Then at the end:
Rujukan:

* Hal. 23 — Fathul Qarib

========================================
2. HUMAN TONE (VERY IMPORTANT)
==============================

Adjust tone to feel like:

* explaining to a friend
* simple, clear, not stiff

Use phrases like:

* "Intinya..."
* "Kalau disederhanakan..."
* "Jadi gampangnya..."

Avoid:

* overly formal or textbook-like tone
* repetitive structured phrasing

========================================
3. FALLBACK BEHAVIOR (LAYER 2)
==============================

Current logic is good, but refine output:

When Muqarrar has no relevant chunks:

1. Start with honesty:
   "Sejauh yang saya temukan, pembahasan itu tidak dijelaskan secara spesifik dalam muqarrar ini."

2. THEN give general fiqh explanation

3. Make sure:

* DO NOT pretend it comes from the kitab
* DO NOT include page references

========================================
4. CITATION FORMAT CLEANUP
==========================

Keep citations ONLY at the end under "Rujukan"

Format:
Rujukan:

* Hal. 23 — Fathul Qarib
* Hal. 24 — Fathul Qarib

Do NOT force citation inside every sentence.
Keep body clean and readable.

========================================
5. DALIL FORMAT (IMPORTANT)
===========================

If Arabic dalil is present:

Wrap it clearly as a separate block.

Example:

Dalil:
[Arabic text here]

Do not mix Arabic text inline with explanation.

========================================
6. SAFETY RULE
==============

Ensure:

* No hallucinated kitab references
* No fake page numbers
* Clear separation:

  * Muqarrar-based answer
  * General knowledge fallback

========================================
7. DO NOT BREAK ANYTHING
========================

* Do NOT change routing logic
* Do NOT change retrieval logic
* Do NOT modify database structure

This is ONLY about:

* response formatting

* output clarity
